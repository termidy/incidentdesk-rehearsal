## Goal

Customer-facing incidents receive the priority adjustment at any hour, while business hours continue to affect routing only.

## Steps

1. Update `incidentdesk/priority.py` so the customer-facing adjustment does not depend on business hours; leave maintenance handling unchanged.
2. Update `tests/unit/test_priority.py` with one test for each example in `docs/priority-rules.md`, including the Tuesday night and Saturday cases.
3. Add a plain-language entry under Unreleased in `CHANGELOG.md`.
4. Leave `incidentdesk/routing.py` and its behavior unchanged.

## Tests

- Tuesday 14:00, major customer-facing `login-service`: assert P1.
- Tuesday 23:30, the same incident: assert P1.
- Saturday 10:00, the same incident: assert P1.
- Run `pytest tests/unit/test_priority.py tests/unit/test_routing.py` to verify priority behavior and unchanged routing.

## Risks

A priority adjustment tied to business hours would continue to under-prioritize night or weekend customer-facing incidents; the explicit table-example tests catch this. Accidentally changing the routing-time distinction could send incidents to the wrong responders; the existing routing tests guard against that.

## Out of scope

Re-prioritizing stored incidents, changing routing, or changing `is_business_hours`.
